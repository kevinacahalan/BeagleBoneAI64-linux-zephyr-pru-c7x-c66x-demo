Over the visible time. A channel's period is the mean from its first rising edge in view to its last, so it takes two; its duty is the time it spends high.
Decoders
A decoder reads the time in view, and draws a row under the channels. SPI takes chip select active low; UART is 8N1; I²C shows starts (S), stops (P), addresses, and acknowledges (A, or N for none). The demo's SPI7 is CS ch3, CLK ch4, MOSI ch5, mode 0.
